# Comprehension quiz (run after the tasks, before any explanation)

Show the participant the sent item (Taylor's reminder) and ask each question; do not rephrase. Score right / wrong / unsure.

| # | Question | Correct answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Right now, has anything actually been *sent* to Taylor?" | Yes — it says it was sent. |
| 2 | "Do we know it *arrived*?" | Yes — the provider confirmed delivery (*Delivery confirmed* is ticked). |
| 3 | "Has Taylor *replied* or sent the documents?" | No — *Outcome seen* is not ticked; it's waiting on the customer. |
| 4 | "Is this job finished?" | No — it stays under *Waiting on others* until there's an outcome. |
| 5 | "What happens if Taylor doesn't answer?" | It comes back into your list (follow-up) — it isn't forgotten. |
| 6 | "Before you pressed Approve, had anything been sent?" | No — it said "Nothing has been sent yet". |
| 7 | "Could you have changed the message after approving and had that version go out?" | No — an approval is for exactly what you reviewed; if it changed you must review again. |
| 8 | "These are made-up customers. Did a real message go out?" | No — practice workspace; nothing was really sent. |

Score = right answers ÷ 8 per person; report **per-question** miss counts (Q3/Q4 are the ones that matter most). Anyone who misses
Q1–Q4 has confused *sent* with *outcome* — treat as a design defect.

**Failed-send variant (optional).** Show *Invoice #1042* after approving: "Was it sent?" Correct: **No** — "Not sent — the connection
needs attention." Any other answer is a false-"done" belief.
