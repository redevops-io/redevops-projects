# Tasks (read aloud, in this order)

Expected behaviour is written against the labels the interface actually uses, so the note-taker can score without guessing.
Say "the workspace", never a screen name. Starting point: the app open on its first screen.

**Warm-up.** "Look around for a moment and tell me, in your own words, what you think this is for."
*Note:* do they say it shows what needs attention? Do they notice the "practice data" banner?

## T1 — Find who is missing a document *(flagship)*
> "A customer is travelling soon and still owes you a visa document. Find out who, and what they still need to send."

**Success:** opens *Visa documents missing — Taylor trip*; says Taylor and the passport scan / visa form. **Fail/assist:** goes to
a menu section instead of the list; cannot find it within ~30s. *Look for:* do they read the "why this matters" and "what I observed"?

## T2 — Review and approve the follow-up *(flagship)*
> "Send Taylor a reminder. Before you do, tell me exactly who it will go to and what it will say."

**Success:** reads the recipient (Taylor Brooks, a number) and the message body aloud from the preview **before** pressing *Approve &
send*; then approves. **Fail:** presses approve without reading; cannot say who it goes to. *Look for:* do they notice the "Nothing has
been sent yet" line and the policy line?

## T3 — Deal with something that isn't urgent
> "The quote for Acme isn't urgent today. Make it stop bothering you until the day after tomorrow."

**Success:** uses *Snooze* (not Dismiss) and chooses a sensible time; the item leaves the decision list. **Fail:** dismisses it, or
cannot find snooze. *Probe:* "Will it come back?" (correct: yes).

## T4 — Was it actually sent? *(flagship)*
> "You approved the reminder to Taylor. Has the message been sent? Has Taylor seen it? Has the problem been solved? How do you know?"

**Success (all three):** sent = yes (the status line says so); delivery confirmed = yes, by the provider; *outcome* = **no, not yet** —
it is *waiting for the customer*. They point to the progress line (Suggested · Approved · Sent · Delivery confirmed · **Outcome seen —
not yet**). **Fail:** says "it's done/solved", or that "sent" means "they replied".

## T5 — Remember something *(capture)*
> "You just heard Jordan still hasn't sent the passport scan. Make a note so you don't forget."

**Success:** types the sentence into the capture field and submits (target: ≤ 2 interactions); understands the confirmation
("Linked to … Nothing has been sent"). *Probe:* "Did that send Jordan anything?" (correct: no).

## T6 — Two people with the same name
> "Send a payment reminder to Sam Rivera."

**Success:** notices it can't send yet and that two people match; picks the right one using the details shown (company / invoice);
then reviews and approves. **Fail:** looks for a way around the block, or picks at random. *Look for:* is the reason understandable?

## T7 — Something goes wrong *(desktop shell only; facilitator stops the runtime)*
> "Carry on with your work." *(facilitator stops the deployment during the task)*

**Success:** notices within ~20s that something changed, says in their own words that it can't reach the system, and — importantly —
believes their work is **not lost**; later sees it come back by itself. **Fail:** thinks the app is frozen/broken, or that they lost
what they did. *Probe:* "What would you do now?"

## Close
"What was the most confusing thing? What did you expect to find that wasn't there? Would you use this instead of what you use today
— why / why not?" Then the quiz.
